Hi, I'm Borano. I study how people read, and build tools that make it easier.

Human-computer interaction researcher and full-stack engineer working at the intersection of typography, accessibility, and applied AI.

I use eye-tracking and pupillometry to understand reading across cognitively and linguistically diverse populations, then ship the software that makes that research possible.

Research

What I work on

How typography and interface design shape reading for people with different cognitive and linguistic profiles, and how AI can help personalize the experience.

End-to-End Machine Learning

Everything from data cleaning and preprocessing to building models from scratch and delivering predictions, with a focus on interpretable deep learning you can actually explain.

Eye-Tracking & Pupillometry

Measuring visual attention and cognitive load during reading, including browser-based, webcam remote studies.

Typography & Readability

How font attributes like size, weight, and spacing affect reading speed, comprehension, and perceived effort.

Inclusive & Accessible Design

Designing for readers with ADHD, dyslexia, and English-as-a-second-language backgrounds across age groups.

Human-Centered AI

Using AI to generate reading primes, summarize documents, and personalize typography at scale.
