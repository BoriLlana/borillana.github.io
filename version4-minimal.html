I study how people read, and build the tools that make it easier.

Human-computer interaction researcher and full-stack engineer working at the intersection of typography, accessibility, and applied AI. I use eye-tracking and pupillometry to understand reading across cognitively and linguistically diverse populations, then ship the software that makes that research possible.
